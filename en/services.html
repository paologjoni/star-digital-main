What Each Service Includes

Website Design & Development

This is our core service: a website built entirely from scratch for your business. We do not start from a purchased template and we do not reuse the previous client's structure. We begin with the question of what a visitor should do when they arrive — call, book, or order — and build the site around that action.

Every project includes custom design, clean code, full adaptation for phone and tablet, load-speed optimization, and the technical SEO structure Google needs to read the site without obstacles. You receive a site ready to publish, not a half-finished project that still needs work.

Landing Pages

A landing page has one job: turning a visitor into a customer. It is the right solution when you are promoting a new product, launching a Facebook or Instagram ad campaign, or want a clear destination for paid traffic that will not get lost inside a busy website.

We build it with a focused message, a clear call to action, and a form that works without errors. Because landing pages are small, they are delivered quickly — often within a few days — so your campaign results can be measured straight away.

How Much Does a Website Cost?

We do not publish a fixed price, because it would be inaccurate for most projects. The cost of a website depends on several concrete factors: how many pages it contains, how many custom features are needed, whether it must be bilingual, whether you sell products online, and how much content has to be produced from scratch.

As a general guide: a single-purpose landing page is the smallest investment, a presentation site for a business with several services sits in the middle, and an online shop with payments and product management is the highest. Redesigning an existing site usually costs less than building from zero.

What we promise is transparency: once you describe the project, you receive a detailed quote showing exactly what is included and what is not. No hidden costs and no extras appearing at the end. Get in touch and we reply within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Do I need to have the text and photos ready?

Not necessarily. If you have them, the process moves faster. If not, we help you structure the content and tell you exactly which photos are needed and in what format. In many cases, missing content is the main reason a project gets delayed.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Yes, if you want to. For sites where content changes often, we build a simple admin panel and show you how to use it. For more static sites, most clients prefer to simply message us and have us make the change.

Will I lose my Google ranking if I rebuild the site?

No, provided the redesign is done properly. We preserve existing page addresses or put accurate redirects in place, so the authority you have built is not lost. In practice, a faster and better-structured site usually ranks better after a rebuild.

Do you build bilingual websites?

Yes. Albanian–English sites are common for businesses serving diaspora customers or tourists. We build them with separate structures for each language rather than machine translation, so both versions can be found on Google.

What happens after the website launches?

The site is entirely yours — domain, hosting, and code. We show you how it works and stay available for questions. If you want ongoing support, the maintenance service covers it; if not, you are under no obligation to continue with us.
