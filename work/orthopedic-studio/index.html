Clinic website

Dr. Pashk Gjoni Orthopedic Studio

A bilingual clinic website that lets patients in Lezhë find, verify and contact their orthopedic specialist online.

Sector
Healthcare
Built with
HTMLCSSJavaScript
Status
Visit live site ↗
01

The challenge

The clinic had no web presence. Patients relied on word of mouth and phone calls for services and hours, and international patients had nothing to read in English.

02

The solution

A fast, lightweight site with a clear page per service, practical clinic information and prominent contact options, fully bilingual so Albanian and English speakers get the same experience. Mobile first, because patients look for a doctor on their phone.

03

The outcome

The clinic can now be found and verified online in two languages. Service and contact details live at one permanent address, and the practice has a foundation for local search visibility.
