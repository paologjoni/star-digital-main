Candles Auroma

An elegant product catalog that gives a handmade candle brand a permanent home beyond its Instagram feed.

Sector

Built with

Status
Visit live site ↗
01

The challenge

Candles Auroma lived entirely on Instagram. Product photos disappeared down the feed, there was no permanent catalog to share, and the brand had no presence that matched the care put into the product.

02

The solution

A refined catalog website built around the product photography: a browsable collection, a gallery and direct ordering through Instagram, keeping the workflow the brand already uses while the website does the presenting.

03

The outcome

The brand now has a considered, shareable home on the web. Customers browse the whole collection in one place and order through a channel the maker already manages daily.
