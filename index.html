Questions

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at €290, business websites at €590 and online stores at €1,190. Adding an AI chatbot to any package is €100. Custom sites, AI assistants and automations are quoted per project. Tell us what you need and you get an exact, free quote within 24 hours.

How long does it take?

Most projects go live in one to three weeks. A landing page can be ready in about a week; larger sites and stores take longer. You get a realistic timeline with your quote.

Do I need to prepare content and photos?

You bring the knowledge of your business, we do the rest. We help structure the content, write and polish the copy, and tell you exactly which photos or materials are worth providing.

Which languages can the site be in?

English, Albanian and German, in any combination. This site runs in all three, and we write and speak all three, so translations are done by us, not by a plugin.

What does the AI chatbot do?

It is trained on your business: services, prices, opening hours, FAQ or product catalog. Visitors ask questions in their own language and get real answers around the clock, and anyone who wants to buy or book is handed to you on WhatsApp or email.

What happens after launch?

The site is yours. We hand over everything, set up hosting and your domain, and offer an optional care plan for updates, security and ongoing improvements.
